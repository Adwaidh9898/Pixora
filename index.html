Compress photos from MB to KB

Large image files can be inconvenient when you need to upload a photo to a form, attach it to a message or publish it on a website. Pixora helps reduce file size using browser-based image encoding.

Choose your desired size, such as 100 KB, 200 KB or 500 KB, and Pixora tries different quality settings to create a smaller image. If the target is especially small, it can also reduce the image dimensions to get closer to your goal.

File size and visual quality depend on the original image, its dimensions, its detail and the output format. An exact target cannot be guaranteed for every photo. Always check the actual output size before using the image for an application or upload.

  • Use a lower target size when you need a smaller file.
  • Use higher quality settings when visual detail matters more.
  • Choose WebP or JPEG depending on your compatibility needs.
  • Keep the original image if you might need its full quality later.

Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers about photo compression, file sizes and privacy.

How can I compress a photo to 100 KB?

Choose your photo, enter 100 in the target size field, select KB and press Compress photo. Pixora will try different quality settings and may resize the image if needed. Check the actual output size before downloading.

Can Pixora reduce an image from MB to KB?

Yes. Pixora can re-encode supported images to reduce their file sizes. The amount of reduction depends on the original image, its dimensions, the chosen format and your quality settings.

Can I compress a photo to exactly 50 KB or 200 KB?

Pixora treats your target as a goal, not a guaranteed exact result. Some images cannot reach a very small size without substantial quality or dimension reduction. The resulting file size is displayed after processing.

Does Pixora support JPG, PNG and WebP?

Pixora accepts JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP images when your browser can decode them. You can export the compressed result as WebP or JPEG. JPEG does not preserve transparent backgrounds, so transparent areas will become white.

Will compressing a photo reduce its quality?

Lossy compression can reduce image quality, especially at very low file sizes. You can adjust the quality slider to balance image appearance and file size. Resizing an image can also reduce detail.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

This page processes selected images locally in your browser using browser image and Canvas features. The compression code does not send your selected photo to a Pixora server.

Can I use Pixora on Android and iPhone?

Yes, on modern mobile browsers that support JavaScript, Canvas and the required image formats. Very large images may use substantial memory on older or lower-powered devices.

Is Pixora free to use?

Yes. This version of Pixora provides its image compression features without requiring an account or payment.
