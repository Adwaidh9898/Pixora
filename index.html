Frequently asked questions

Useful answers before you compress.

How do I compress a photo from MB to KB?

Upload your photo, select a target such as 500 KB, and press Compress Photo. Pixora re-encodes the image and measures the actual result.

Can I choose the exact file size?

You can set a target size, but an exact match is not always possible. The result depends on the image content, format, quality and dimensions.

Will my photo lose quality?

Lossy formats such as JPEG and WebP can lose some detail. Pixora measures output sizes and applies quality limits; aggressive compression may also reduce dimensions.

Does Pixora upload my photos?

No. This version processes images locally in your browser and does not send image files to a Pixora server. Browser extensions and your device environment are outside the app's control.

Can I compress PNG images?

Yes. PNG can be re-encoded, but its lossless encoding may not shrink a photograph much. JPEG or WebP can be smaller but may remove transparency.

Does it work on Android?

Yes, in modern mobile browsers with Canvas and image decoding support. Very large images may exceed the available memory on some devices.

Why did my photo become larger?

Some source images are already highly optimized. Re-encoding them may increase their size. Pixora reports the actual output size rather than claiming a saving.

Why could Pixora not reach my target?

Some images cannot safely reach very small sizes at acceptable quality. Try enabling target-priority mode, allowing resizing, or choosing a larger target.

Will image dimensions change?

They can change when automatic resizing is enabled or when a maximum dimension is selected. Aspect ratio is maintained and images are never intentionally upscaled.

Does compression remove image metadata?

Canvas re-encoding usually removes EXIF metadata, including GPS and camera information. Do not use this tool if you need the original metadata preserved.

Which formats are supported?

This version accepts JPEG, PNG and WebP files. The browser must be able to decode the image. Output formats depend on the browser's Canvas encoder.
